
Medius E-Reporting automates and tracks the transmission of transaction and payment data to the French tax authority (DGFiP) as required by the French electronic invoicing reform, effective September 2026. It covers B2B international transactions and B2C operations - those outside the scope of e-invoicing - ensuring full regulatory coverage.

This streamlines your tax reporting process and keeps your business compliant with French regulations.
