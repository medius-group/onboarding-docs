Before you start
----------------

You are about to complete the E-Invoicing Company Onboarding in the **Test Environment**. This is a non-production flow, no data will be registered with the PPF and no changes will impact your production tenant.

**What you'll need:**

- SIREN number: the 9-digit identifier shared by the companies you want to onboard.
- Routing suffixes for each company.

**What you'll do:**

- Select all companies sharing the same SIREN number and onboard them together.
- Review and confirm the routing suffixes for each company.
