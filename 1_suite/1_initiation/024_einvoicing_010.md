
Medius Accounts Receivable lets you issue and send compliant French e-invoices through the Medius API, with Medius acting as your Accredited Platform (PA). It covers all outbound documents on the network. This includes every French document type: standard invoices, credit notes, pre-payment invoices, and corrected invoices.

You can send in whichever way fits your ERP: submit a ready-made file in a French-mandated format (UBL, CII, or Factur-X), or have Medius generate the invoice for you from structured data when your ERP can't produce one. Once an invoice is out, you can track its status in both directions, network and business events coming back to you, and the seller-side statuses you emit, and see the whole exchange at a glance in the built-in Monitoring tool.
