
Before you start
----------------

To activate e-invoicing under the French e-invoicing reform for your company, we are required by law to verify your identity and confirm that you are authorised to act on behalf of this business.
Before you start, please have ready:

- A valid ID document — passport or national ID card.
- Proof of authority — extrait Kbis (less than 3 months old), or a signed power of attorney.
- Company information — Legal form, registered address and contact email.
- VAT details — TVA intracommunautaire number, VAT regime, IBAN and BIC.

Make sure that all the information you provide is accurate and up to date.
