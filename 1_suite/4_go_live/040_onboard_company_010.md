Before you start
----------------

You have selected Medius as your Partner Platform (PA) for the French e-invoicing reform. Before you start, please make sure you have the following ready.

**What you'll need:**

- SIREN number: the 9-digit identifier shared by the companies you want to onboard.
- Company details: legal form, registered address, and contact email for each company.
- A valid identity document: passport or national ID card (required for identity verification).
- Proof of authority: extrait Kbis (less than 3 months old) or a signed power of attorney, confirming you are authorised to act on behalf of the business.

**What you'll do:**

- Select all companies sharing the same SIREN number and onboard them together.
- Review and confirm the routing suffixes for each company.
- Complete the identity verification process.

Make sure all the information you provide is accurate and up to date.
