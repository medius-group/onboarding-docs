Bring Your Own PA
-----------------

<WarningTag padBottom title="You are not using Medius as your Partner Platform." text="If you think this is incorrect, contact your implementation partner before continuing." />

You are onboarding with your own Partner Platform (PA). Medius will not manage your e-invoicing registration. Ensure your PA has completed your registration on the PPF before proceeding.

**What you'll need to provide:**

- SIREN code: the 9-digit identifier of the company/ies you onboarded.
- Electronic addresses: the e-invoicing addresses you registered when onboarding with your PA (For both APA and Expense Management if applicable).

**What you'll do:**

- Select all companies sharing the same SIREN number.
- Provide the electronic addresses for each company.
- Save your input.

Make sure all the information you provide is accurate and up to date.
